## What this says, and what it does not

- **The model's price is not what decides the economics.** A model call costs about USD 0.001409 per attempted case: USD 1.42 a month for all of today's text contacts, against USD 293–1,172 of agent time avoided. Halving or doubling the price changes nothing visible.
- **Volume and the safe-resolution rate decide it.** Today's text traffic yields about 58.6 agent hours a month, 14.2% of the transactional hours: small in absolute terms. The lever the data points to is the channel mix, not the model: phone is 85% of this demand.
- **The scenarios do not say that phone customers would move.** The shift is an assumption, voice is not built, and nothing in the data measures willingness to change channel.
- **The safe-resolution rate is measured on synthetic, team-written held-out cases** and may not hold on production traffic. Its interval is wide because only 60 of the live cases are in scope.
- **Agent cost per hour is assumed**, shown as a range, and escalated contacts keep costing what they cost today, so only the automated ones count as saved.
